I'm Shanvi Chaudhary, a second-year B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering at Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology. I am curious about how technology works, but even more interested in how it can make everyday experiences simpler, clearer, and more thoughtful.

I enjoy turning an early idea into something people can actually use. My journey so far has moved through Python, Java, web development, and interface design. I am building my fundamentals one project at a time, learning to think through problems patiently and create with intention.
